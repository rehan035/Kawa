## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the heritage, craftsmanship, and raw industrial capability of Sialkot’s world-renowned leather tanning and fabrication sector. It bridges the gap between old-world bench craft, commercial B2B contract manufacturing, and direct factory-outlet commerce. The tone is authoritative, tactile, disciplined, and uncompromisingly authentic. It balances the artisanal warmth of hand-dyed saddle hide with the structural precision of high-output export industrialism.

### Target Audience
- International wholesale buyers, luxury apparel procurers, private-label brands, and bespoke retail patrons seeking direct source-origin goods.
- Institutional clients who prioritize material authenticity, verified export standards, hide grading transparency, and tannery provenance over mass-market retail sheen.

### Emotional Response
The interface must instill an immediate visceral impression of oiled grain, heavy brass hardware, drafting tables, and disciplined warehouse ledgers. Users should feel they are stepping directly into an export atelier—grounded, prestigious, and clear-headed.

### Aesthetic Movement & Execution
- **Industrial Luxury & Functional Brutalism**: Strict architectural alignment, razor-sharp edge definition, explicit drafting rules, and tabular layout balance.
- **Editorial Archival Warmth**: Rich tactile ivory canvases instead of sterile digital white, warm espresso instead of pure digital black, and muted burnished brass details acting as functional proof marks.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette draws straight from the workshop floor: raw vegetable-tanned crust, burnished saddlery edges, machine-cast brass buckles, and archival paper stock. 

- **Primary (`#1F1A17` - Tannery Espresso)**: The bedrock tone. Replaces synthetic `#000000` with deep iron-mordanted bark black, applied to dominant typography, rigid frames, and high-impact structural borders.
- **Secondary (`#703816` - Cognac Saddle Hide)**: The core brand accent. Evokes rich pull-up hides, oil-finished aniline leathers, and workshop edge stain. Used for primary CTAs, active status badges, and focal interactive elements.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059` - Burnished Brass)**: Precision accent tone representing hardware rivets, lot inspection stamps, serial tags, and calibrated technical gauges. Used sparingly to denote verification, export certification, and premium attributes.
- **Neutral (`#F5F0EB` - Warm Milling Ivory)**: Background canvas with subtle warmth, mimicking unbleached parchment and workshop inspection dockets. Paired with pure surface highlights (`#FDFBF7`) for interior containers.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas**: `#F5F0EB` (Dense, tactile background).
- **Surface Elevation 01**: `#FDFBF7` (Crisp container background, parchment tone).
- **Surface Inset / Muted**: `#EBE3D7` (Technical tables, filter sidebars, invoice headers).
- **Border / Rule Tone**: `#D8CEBF` (Calibrated ghost rule) and `#1F1A17` (High-contrast structural divider).
- **Typography - Heavy**: `#1F1A17` (Headlines, SKU labels, critical metrics).
- **Typography - Muted**: `#544840` (Specs, technical body, certification metadata).

## Typography

### Structural Concept
The typographical framework balances classical European luxury atelier provenance with German/Swiss trade efficiency:

1. **EB Garamond (Display & Headline)**: Evokes archival ledger headers, hallmark stamps, and historical export registries. Used exclusively for brand manifestos, high-level tier markers, section titles, and product lineage titles.
2. **Hanken Grotesk (Body, Spec Sheets, & Labels)**: Clean, unembellished neo-grotesque type engineered for precise legibility across technical tables, hide gauge readings, minimum order volume formulas, and UI controls.

### Typesetting Rules
- All `label-*` tiers are rendered strictly in uppercase when used for statuses, data categories, and column identifiers to evoke factory-stamped crates and serial placards.
- Numbers in B2B spec sheets, lot batches, pricing tiers, and dimensional listings should leverage tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain strict vertical column alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The layout mirrors an industrial cutting mat and export manifest. It favors deliberate, visible 1px borders dividing modules over floating ambiguous cards. Layouts are strictly structured around a 12-column grid system that maintains structural rigidity across viewport transitions.

### Grid Construction
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12 columns, max content envelope `1440px`. Column gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`). Outer canvas margins: `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8 columns. Column gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`). Outer margins: `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (≤ 767px)**: 4 columns. Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`). Canvas margin: `1rem` (`16px`). Content modules flatten into stacked horizontal ledgers.

### Module Rules
- Sections must be demarcated by horizontal hairline rules (`1px solid #D8CEBF`) rather than arbitrary whitespace blocks.
- Dense information (e.g., Hide Grade, Temper, Substance/Thickness, Tanning Method, Port of Origin, Bulk MOQ) should be organized into rigid tabular grids that span precise column divisions (e.g., 2-column key-value metrics).

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Zero Virtual Elevation
This design system avoids soft, floating SaaS shadows and simulated drop-shadows. Quality is expressed through the material tactility of bordering, sharp layering, and ink-trapping. The tactile depth is that of folded, die-cut cardstock and stamped leather swatches.

### Depth Mechanisms
1. **Structural Tonal Insets**: Depth is created by placing `#FDFBF7` containers over the `#F5F0EB` base field, framed with a continuous `1px` border of `#D8CEBF`.
2. **Hard Framing**: When interactive elements (such as modal sheets or bulk ordering drawers) overlay the interface, they use hard, dark framing (`1px solid #1F1A17`) paired with a sharp, offset industrial shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.85);`
3. **Ghost Inset Borders**: Inactive fields, metric tiles, and secondary components utilize `1px solid rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.12)` to maintain grid discipline without visual clutter.
4. **Interactive Hover Depth**: On active state or mouse hover, components snap outward using a crisp mechanical offset rather than a blurred elevation lift:
   - `transform: translate(-1px, -1px); box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px 0px #1F1A17;`

## Shapes

### Edge Discipline: Absolute Precision (`roundedness: 0`)
To honor industrial cutting knives, die-cut patterns, heavy leather shears, and export shipping manifests, rounded corners are strictly prohibited. 

- All buttons, inputs, cards, notification banners, swatch chips, and structural panels must have `0px` border-radius (`border-radius: 0`).
- No rounded pills or circular tags. Badges are styled as clipped rectangular cut-outs or stamped technical chits.
- Dividers between table cells and header ribbons must form crisp 90-degree right angles, evoking steel tooling and technical drafting plates.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary CTA**: Solid `#703816` background, crisp white/cream `#FDFBF7` uppercase text, `0px` border radius, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Frame with `1px solid #703816`. On hover: transition to `#1F1A17` background with a mechanical snap.
- **Secondary / Export Inquiry**: Solid `#FDFBF7` background, `1px solid #1F1A17` frame, `#1F1A17` text. On hover: reverse out to `#1F1A17` background with `#FDFBF7` text.
- **Tertiary / Link Action**: Inline typographic link in bold uppercase `label-md` with continuous `1px` underline in `#C5A059`, offset by `4px`.

### Product & Sample Cards
- **Construction**: Never an isolated floating island. Cards are connected via shared 1px borders in an editorial sheet layout.
- **Image Container**: Aspect ratio strictly `4:5` (apparel/bags) or `1:1` (hides/leather swatches). Background `#EBE3D7`. Images must showcase grain macro details, edge finishing, and hardware under controlled, directional studio lighting.
- **Meta Band**: Bottom section of card displays tabular data:
  - Header: Article Code & Leather Type (e.g., *ART-802 | Pull-Up Cowhide 1.2–1.4mm*).
  - Footer: Factory direct price / B2B tier bracket separated by a solid vertical hairline.

### Chips & Specification Tags
- **Appearance**: Flat rectangular badges with `border: 1px solid #D8CEBF`, uppercase `label-sm` font, padding `2px 8px`.
- **Status Indicators**:
  - *In Stock (Outlet)*: `#F5F0EB` fill, `#1F1A17` text, left border `3px solid #703816`.
  - *Contract Tanning / Export Only*: Background `#F5F0EB`, text `#544840`, left border `3px solid #C5A059`.
  - *Defect Grade / Sample Lot*: Text `#9C3A27`, left border `3px solid #9C3A27`.

### Lists & Technical Data Tables
- **Table Headers**: Background `#EBE3D7`, uppercase `label-md` in `#1F1A17`, text-align left, border-bottom `2px solid #1F1A17`.
- **Rows**: Alternating subtle hover states (`#FDFBF7` to `#F8F4EE`). Row borders are `1px solid #D8CEBF`. Height fixed to compact `44px` for high-density B2B quoting.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Field Inputs
- **Input Fields**: Crisp `1px solid #1F1A17` boundary, background `#FDFBF7`, square edges. Typography `body-md`. Focus state: thick `2px solid #703816` ring with zero soft glow. Placeholder text in `#8C827A`.
- **Selection Controls**: Square check boxes (`16px x 16px`), zero border-radius. Active state filled with `#703816` containing a crisp geometric checkmark. Radio buttons are square toggles with an inner solid square indicator rather than rounded circles.

### Specialized Factory Components
- **Hide Gauge Indicator**: A horizontal micro-chart illustrating leather thickness (e.g., `0.8 - 1.0mm`, `1.2 - 1.4mm`, `1.8 - 2.0mm`) utilizing brass-colored segment bars (`#C5A059`).
- **Tannery Origin Stamp**: A verified visual seal with `EB Garamond` italicized subtext, `1px solid #C5A059` border, certifying ethical chrome-free/vegetable tanning protocols compliant with European REACH standards.
